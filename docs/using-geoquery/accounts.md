# Accounts and request history

You don't need an account to use GeoQuery: you can submit a request with just an email address. An account keeps all your requests in one place.

## Signing in

GeoQuery accounts use GitHub. Open the **Account** menu in the header, choose **Sign in**, and sign in with your GitHub account. The first time, you choose which of your GitHub account's email addresses to connect to GeoQuery.

Once you're signed in:

- New requests are submitted under your account's email address, so you don't need to type it.
- **My Requests**, in the **Account** menu, lists every request made with your connected email addresses.

## Linking requests you made before signing in

Requests are linked to your account by email address. To bring in requests you made before you had an account, including those from earlier versions of GeoQuery, open the **Account** page and add the email address you used. Once you've verified it, those requests appear in your history automatically.

## Finding past requests without an account

Open **Past Requests** in the **Account** menu, enter the email address you submitted requests with, and we'll email you a personal link to your request history. The link stays valid for about six months; after that, request a new one the same way.

## Checking on a request

Each request has its own page showing its status, which datasets and boundaries it includes, and a download link once it's ready. The email we send when you submit a request links to it, and so does your request history.

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **materializing** | We've received your request and are setting up the work it needs. |
| **ingesting** | We're reading the boundary file you uploaded. |
| **queued** | Waiting for its turn to be processed. |
| **processing** | Some of its data is still being extracted. We check again regularly until it's all ready. |
| **preparing** | The data is ready and your download is being put together. |
| **completed** | Ready to download. We've emailed you the link. |
| **error** | Something went wrong. Email [geo@aiddata.wm.edu](mailto:geo@aiddata.wm.edu) with the request's ID (the last part of its page address) and we'll look into it. |

If a request is taking longer than you expected, [System status](system-status.md) shows how busy GeoQuery is right now.

## Sharing and reusing a request

A request's page can be opened by anyone with its link, so you can send it to collaborators to see what you asked for and download the results.

To build a new request from one you or someone else made, click **Use as template** on its page. See [Starting from a past request](getting-started.md#starting-from-a-past-request), and [Sharing a request](getting-started.md#sharing-a-request) for sharing one you haven't submitted yet.

## Requests from earlier versions of GeoQuery

Requests made on earlier versions of GeoQuery are kept, and appear in your request history alongside new ones. Their pages show the original details and download.
