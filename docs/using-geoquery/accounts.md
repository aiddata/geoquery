# Accounts and request history

You don't need an account to use GeoQuery: you can submit a request with just an email address. An account keeps all your requests in one place.

## Signing in

GeoQuery accounts use GitHub. Click **Sign in** in the header and sign in with your GitHub account. The first time, you choose which of your GitHub account's email addresses to connect to GeoQuery.

Once you're signed in:

- New requests are submitted under your account's email address, so you don't need to type it.
- **Past Requests** lists every request made with your connected email addresses.

## Linking requests you made before signing in

Requests are linked to your account by email address. To bring in requests you made before you had an account, including those from earlier versions of GeoQuery, open the **Account** page and add the email address you used. Once you've verified it, those requests appear in your history automatically.

## Finding past requests without an account

Open **Past Requests**, enter the email address you submitted requests with, and we'll email you a personal link to your request history. The link stays valid for about six months; after that, request a new one the same way.

## Checking on a request

Each request has its own page showing its progress, which datasets and boundaries it includes, and a download link once it's ready. The email we send when you submit a request links to it, and so does your request history.

## Requests from earlier versions of GeoQuery

Requests made on earlier versions of GeoQuery are kept, and appear in your request history alongside new ones. Their pages show the original details and download.
