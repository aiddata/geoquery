# GeoQuery

**GeoQuery** is a free platform developed by [AidData](https://www.aiddata.org/geoquery) at William & Mary that makes it easy to extract geospatial data for any area of interest — no GIS expertise required.

Select boundaries, choose from a curated library of datasets, and download results ready for analysis in Excel, Stata, SPSS, R, or GIS software.

[Open GeoQuery](https://geoquery.aiddata.wm.edu){ .md-button .md-button--primary } [Get started →](using-geoquery/getting-started.md){ .md-button }

---

## How it works

1. **Select your boundaries**: choose from administrative boundaries for countries around the world, as well as other boundary sets. Uploading your own boundaries is coming soon.
2. **Choose datasets**: browse datasets spanning population, economic activity, climate, the environment, health, conflict and more, and pick the measures (such as mean or maximum) and time periods you need.
3. **Submit your request**: enter your email (or sign in) and submit. We'll email you a link to download your results when they're ready.
4. **Analyze your data**: download a CSV with one row per feature, the boundaries as a GeoPackage for GIS software, and a metadata file with each dataset's source and citation.
