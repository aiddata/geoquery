# Privacy Policy

!!! warning "Draft"

    This policy is a draft awaiting review. It describes how GeoQuery works today, but its wording may change before it is final.

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

GeoQuery is a free service run by AidData, a research lab at William & Mary. This policy explains what information GeoQuery collects when you use the GeoQuery application and this documentation site, and what we do with it.

## What we collect

**When you submit a request**

- The email address you enter, which we use to send you your results.
- The details of your request: the boundaries and datasets you chose, the options you selected, and the name you gave it.

**When you sign in with GitHub**

- Your GitHub account's ID and username.
- The email addresses from your GitHub account that you choose to connect, and any others you add and verify on your Account page.

**When you use the site**

- **Usage analytics.** We use Google Analytics on the application and on this documentation site to understand how they're used, for example which pages are visited. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your browser, device and approximate location.
- **Server logs.** Like most websites, our servers record technical information about each visit, such as your IP address, the page requested and the time, for security and troubleshooting.
- **Map tiles.** The background map is loaded from Protomaps, which receives your IP address when your browser requests map tiles.

## Cookies and browser storage

- **Essential cookies** keep you signed in and protect forms from misuse.
- **Analytics cookies** are set by Google Analytics. You can block them in your browser settings or with an extension, and GeoQuery still works.
- **Browser storage** remembers your preferences on your own device, such as your light or dark theme and whether you've dismissed a notice. It isn't sent to us.

## How we use your information

- To process your requests and email you when results are ready.
- To show you your request history.
- To answer you when you contact us.
- To understand how GeoQuery is used and improve it. We publish aggregate figures, such as numbers of requests and the most requested datasets, on the [statistics page](https://geoquery.aiddata.wm.edu/stats). These never identify you.
- To report on GeoQuery's reach to AidData's funders and partners, for example the number of organizations using it, based on the domains of users' email addresses. These reports are aggregate and don't name individuals.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of GeoQuery for us, and only as needed for them to do so:

- **GitHub**, when you sign in with your GitHub account. This documentation site is also hosted on GitHub Pages.
- **Google**, for usage analytics and to send GeoQuery's emails.
- **Cloudflare**, which delivers the GeoQuery application's pages and files.
- **Protomaps**, for map tiles.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

## How long we keep it

We keep your requests and their results for as long as GeoQuery operates, so that your request history and downloads keep working, and so that results can be reused for later requests. Account information is kept until you ask us to delete your account. Server logs are kept for a limited time.

## Your choices

- **Deleting your information.** Email us at [geo@aiddata.wm.edu](mailto:geo@aiddata.wm.edu) to ask us to delete your account or remove your email address from your requests. We may keep the requests themselves, without your email address, for aggregate statistics.
- **GitHub access.** You can revoke GeoQuery's access to your GitHub account at any time in your GitHub settings, under Applications.
- **Analytics.** You can block analytics cookies in your browser.

## Children

GeoQuery is intended for researchers and practitioners, and is not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and post about significant changes on the [Updates](updates/index.md) page.
